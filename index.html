MEDICAL MULTIMODAL LEARNINGSEP 2025 — MAY 2026

Multimodal Survival Prediction
Based on Foundation Models

Led a project combining whole-slide pathology images, genomic data, and pathology-report text to predict patient survival risk.

  • Aligned TCGA whole-slide images, genomics, clinical reports, and survival labels across five cancer cohorts.
  • Extracted image features with UNI, encoded genomic data with SNN, and processed reports with BioClinicalBERT.
  • Extended the MOTCat pathology-gene backbone to a tri-modal framework with a gated text-refinement module.
+13.3%relative C-index improvement on UCEC versus MOTCat; highest C-index among evaluated models on BRCA, UCEC, and GBMLGG.
